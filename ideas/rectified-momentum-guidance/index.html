    Rectified Momentum Guidance for Flow Models流模型上的修正动量引导

    The Problem问题

    Guidance is usually explained as tilting the final distribution by a reward. REG showed that this explanation does not hold: rescaling the marginals conflicts with the reverse process, and the valid objective rescales the joint distribution of the whole chain. The exact correction at time t then depends on the expected final reward given the current state. Existing guidance methods cannot see that far ahead, so they use a local signal instead, and REG narrows the gap with a Jacobian correction. Guidance 通常被解释成用一个 reward 去调整最终分布。REG 证明这个解释并不成立：直接缩放边缘分布和反向过程矛盾，合法的目标是缩放整条链的联合分布。这样一来，t 时刻的精确修正取决于给定当前状态时最终 reward 的期望。现有的 guidance 看不到这么远，只能用局部信号代替，REG 再用一个 Jacobian 修正缩小这个差距。

    Momentum Guidance (MG) should inherit this gap in a harder form. It extrapolates the current velocity away from an average of past velocities, which improves samples at one model evaluation per step. But it states no reward or target distribution, and each step ignores how its change propagates to the final sample. Momentum Guidance（MG）应该也有这个差距，而且更难处理。它让当前速度远离过去速度的平均，每步只调用一次模型就能提升样本质量。但它没有给出 reward 或目标分布，每一步也没有考虑这次改动会怎样传到最终样本。

    The Idea想法

    I want to give MG a reward-based theory, then derive a rectified version of it. This is more than applying REG directly, for two reasons. MG carries a momentum state, so its sampler is not Markov in the current sample alone, while REG's theory is built on a Markov chain. REG's correction also differentiates through the network, which costs extra runtime and memory and would give up MG's main advantage. However, the velocities MG already stores along the path carry derivative information, so part of this correction may come for free. 我想先给 MG 建立基于 reward 的理论，再推出它的修正版本。这不是把 REG 直接套上去，原因有两个。MG 带着动量状态，只看当前样本时它的采样过程不是马尔可夫的，而 REG 的理论建立在马尔可夫链上。REG 的修正还要对网络求导，会增加运行时间和内存，这就丢掉了 MG 的主要优势。不过 MG 沿轨迹存下的速度本身带有导数信息，这个修正也许有一部分可以免费得到。

    Open Questions开放问题

    Three questions are open. What reward or path objective does MG implicitly follow, given its momentum state? For a deterministic flow ODE, where the current state fixes the final sample, what is the exact correction? Can stored velocities approximate REG's Jacobian term, so that the rectified sampler still uses one model evaluation per step? The goal is a guidance method with a clear target that improves on MG at the same cost. 现在有三个开放问题：MG 带着动量状态，它隐含的 reward 或路径目标是什么？在确定性的流 ODE 里，当前状态决定了最终样本，精确的修正是什么？存下的速度能否近似 REG 的 Jacobian 项，让修正后的采样器仍然每步只调用一次模型？目标是得到一个目标清楚、在同样成本下比 MG 更好的 guidance 方法。

    Key Papers关键论文

    • REG: Rectified Gradient Guidance for Conditional Diffusion Models (Gao et al., ICML 2025) — the joint-scaling objective, the exact correction and the Jacobian term. — 联合分布缩放的目标、精确修正和 Jacobian 项。
    • Momentum Guidance: Plug-and-Play Guidance for Flow Models (Liao et al., 2026) — guidance from past velocities at one model evaluation per step. — 用过去的速度做 guidance，每步只调用一次模型。
    • Classifier-Free Diffusion Guidance (Ho & Salimans, 2022) — the reward interpretation of guidance that REG corrects. — REG 所修正的 guidance 的 reward 解释。
    • Classifier-Free Guidance is a Predictor-Corrector (Bradley & Nakkiran, 2024) — CFG does not sample from the tilted distribution it is said to target. — CFG 并不是从它声称的调整后分布中采样。
    • Guiding a Diffusion Model with a Bad Version of Itself (Karras et al., NeurIPS 2024) — guidance from a weaker reference branch. — 用更弱的参考分支做 guidance。
    • Flow Straight and Fast (Liu et al., ICLR 2023) — Rectified Flow, the framework MG builds on. — Rectified Flow，MG 所基于的框架。
